# Fonts

`DejaVuSans*.ttf` and `DejaVuSansMono*.ttf` are the **DejaVu fonts** (derived from Bitstream Vera), used to draw the
calendar. They are free to use, modify and redistribute under the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license, which requires that
the copyright and permission notice travel with copies. The full text is at <https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html>
and in the upstream release archive (`LICENSE`). They are included here unmodified.
